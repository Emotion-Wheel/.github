# Emotion Wheel — Your Interactive Guide to Understanding Your Feelings

In a world where stress, overwhelm, and emotional confusion are part of everyday life, **[Emotion Wheel](https://emotionswheel.app/?utm_source=github)** gives you a moment to pause and truly check in with yourself. Built around the classic **Feelings Wheel**, this interactive tool helps you slow down, name what you're experiencing, and understand what your emotions are trying to tell you — without judgment.

Unlike the static emotion wheel images scattered across the internet, our platform is a fully interactive experience. Click any emotion to instantly unlock its explanation, how it shows up in your mind and body, and gentle, practical ways to support yourself in the moment.

---

## How It Works

1. **Pause and check in** — notice what you're feeling and what you sense in your body.
2. **Choose a core emotion** from the center of the wheel (happy, sad, angry, etc.).
3. **Move outward** to find the more specific feeling that fits best.
4. **Name it without judgment** — simply acknowledge what's there.
5. **Read the explanation** to understand what this emotion might be signaling (a crossed boundary, an unmet need, or something else).
6. **Try a gentle practice** to help yourself feel a little better.

---

## What You'll Find Inside

- 🧠 **What this emotion is** — a short, clear explanation of the feeling and what it's about.
- 💭 **How it feels in your mind and body** — so you can recognize it more easily.
- 🎯 **What it's useful for** — emotions are signals; learn what yours is pointing to.
- ⚠️ **What to pay attention to** — what can happen if the emotion is ignored over time.
- 🌬️ **Simple regulation practices** — breathing, grounding, and other quick self-help techniques tailored to the emotion.
- 🌿 **Aromatherapy suggestions** — essential oils linked to your current mood to help you feel calmer or more energized.
